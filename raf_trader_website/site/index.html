FAQ

Procurement, Answered.

“Couldn’t find what you were looking for? Write to us at info@raftraders.com”

What makes RAF Traders' machines different from others?

Our machines are imported directly from Europe on an as-is basis, ensuring buyers get genuine, unaltered equipment. We personally inspect and evaluate every unit before import, so you know exactly what you're getting.

What types of machines do you supply?

We deal in used offset presses, prepress and postpress equipment, packaging machines, guillotines, web offset machines, screen printing setups, rotogravure machines, and more.

How do you ensure quality?

Every machine is inspected and evaluated by our team before import. We only select units that meet our strict standards for condition and performance.

Are your machines original and unaltered?

Yes. We never refurbish, repaint, or modify any machine. Every unit arrives exactly as it left its country of origin, with all original parts intact.

Do you offer machines suited to specific production needs?

Absolutely. Our team helps buyers identify the right machine based on their production capacity and requirements.

How competitive are your prices?

Since our machines are imported used and sold as-is, we offer significantly better pricing compared to new equipment, without compromising on genuine quality.
